## What to include

Include these details so we can help fast:

- Your Jira site URL, such as `your-company.atlassian.net`.
- The steps you took before the problem appeared.
- What you expected to happen.
- What happened instead.
- Screenshots, if the problem is visual.
- The app version, if you know it.

## Bug reports

Found a bug? Tell us what broke and how to reproduce it.
We fix bugs fast. Your report shapes the roadmap.

## Feature requests

Need a feature? Tell us what you want and why.
Your feedback shapes the roadmap.
