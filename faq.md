## What is TrueTime for Jira?

TrueTime is Forge-native timesheets for Jira.
Log time on issues. See weeks at a glance. Approve weeks with one click.

## Where does my time data live?

In your Jira. Worklogs are native Jira worklogs.
Only a running timer lives in Forge Storage.
TrueTime has no servers. We cannot see, sell, or lose your data.

## How much does TrueTime cost?

Free for up to 10 users. $2.99 per user per month above that.
You pay per person who tracks time, not per Jira seat.
Example: 200 Jira seats, 15 trackers. You pay for 15.

## How do I migrate from Tempo?

Export Tempo data to CSV. Import the file into TrueTime.
Your history moves with you. Read the [migration guide](/truetime/migration.html).

## Does TrueTime work on Jira Server or Data Center?

No. TrueTime is Cloud only.

## What happens to my data if I uninstall?

Worklogs stay in Jira. Billable flags stay in Jira.
A running timer is lost. Nothing else changes.

## How fast is support?

We reply within 1 business day, Monday to Friday, Central Time, except US holidays.
Paid subscribers get priority. See our [support SLA](/sla.html).

## Is TrueTime in beta?

Yes. Features may change. Full release is planned for Q4 2026.
[Join the beta](/beta.html).
