Tempo is the big timesheet app for Jira. TrueTime is the Forge-native alternative.
This page compares the two on facts that matter to buyers.

| Topic | TrueTime | Tempo |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly price | $2.99 per time-tracker, free to 10 users | $5.21 per Jira seat |
| Billing base | People who track time | Every Jira seat |
| Data location | Atlassian only (Forge) | Vendor servers |
| Platform | Jira Cloud only | Jira Cloud and Data Center |
| Support first response | Within 1 business day | Within 1 business day |
| Team delete | 30-day trash with restore | Archive, no restore |
| Approvals | Simple submit, approve, reject | Full approval workflows |
| Migration | Import Tempo CSV in minutes | Export CSV any time |

Example: 200 Jira seats, 15 trackers. Tempo costs $1,042 per month. TrueTime costs $45 per month.

Tempo prices and SLA come from its public Marketplace listing and support pages, checked October 2026.
Tempo wins on platform range and approval depth. TrueTime wins on price, billing base, and data location.
