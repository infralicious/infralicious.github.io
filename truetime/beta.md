TrueTime is in beta. Full release is planned for Q4 2026.

## Who fits

- Teams that track time in Jira.
- Tempo users who want a cheaper, Forge-native option.
- Teams that accept rough edges and want to shape the release.

## How to join

Use the [contact form](/contact.html). Say "beta access" in your message.
We will send install steps after you ask.

## What feedback helps

- Bugs, with steps to reproduce them.
- Migration pain, with your Tempo export shape.
- Missing features, with your use case.

Beta means features may change. Worklogs stay in your Jira either way.
