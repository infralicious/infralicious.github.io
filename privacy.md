# Privacy Policy for Infralicious

**Last updated:** 2026-10-18

Infralicious ("we", "us") builds software for teams.
Contact: ***REMOVED***

## What This Policy Covers

This policy covers all apps that Infralicious builds.
Each app also has its own policy page.
The app page lists the data that the app stores.
This page states the shared rules.

## What Data We Collect

Our apps run on the platforms you already use.
They store data on that platform's infrastructure.
We operate no separate database for app data.

Our apps do not collect analytics.
Our apps use no tracking pixels.
Our apps use no cookies for advertising.
We do not sell your data.
We do not share your data with third parties.

## Where Data Is Stored

App data stays on the host platform's infrastructure.
For Atlassian apps, data stays in Atlassian systems.
We send no app data to servers outside the host platform.

## Data Retention

Each app deletes its stored data when you uninstall it.
The host platform controls the deletion.
See the app policy page for app-specific retention rules.

## Your Rights (GDPR/CCPA)

- **Access:** You can view your data in the app.
- **Deletion:** Uninstall the app to delete its stored data.
- **Portability:** Each app offers export where it applies.
- **No sale:** We do not sell, share, or disclose your data to third parties.

## Third Parties

We use no third-party analytics, advertising, or data processing services for app data.

## Security

Our apps run within the host platform's sandbox.
We inherit the host platform's security certifications.
We store no credentials for app data access.

## Changes

We will update this policy when our data practices change.
Material changes will be announced via the app listing.
